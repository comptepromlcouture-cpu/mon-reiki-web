## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a refined 3D ivory ceramic meditation figurine for a wellness app, fully dressed in a simple loose ivory long-sleeved tunic and trousers. Front view, sitting cross-legged, hands resting on knees, symmetrical posture, upright torso. Smooth anonymous face, short smooth hair, calm minimal sculptural style. Warm pearl and champagne material, soft diffuse light, subtle translucency in ceramic edges. Full figure visible including head, hands, crossed legs. Square composition, centered, figure fills 82 percent of height. Genuine transparent background, no scenery, no UI, no symbols, no text, no large halo, only a faint contact shadow. Clearly defined silhouette for overlaying chakra markers in the app.
